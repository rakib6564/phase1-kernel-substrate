Our story

One pit. One obsession. Zero shortcuts.

Chef Gregory built his first offset smoker in a backyard in 2009. Fifteen years later we still run on the same principle: real wood, real time, and meat we'd be proud to serve our own family.

No gas assist, no liquid smoke, no rushing. Just post oak, a thermometer, and the patience to wait until it's right.
